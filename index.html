About Us

Why Choose Petal & Co.

We started Petal & Co. with one simple idea: flowers should feel alive, not like they came off a factory line. Every bouquet is cut the same day, arranged by hand, and checked for freshness before it leaves our shop.

From birthdays to weddings, we've delivered over 5,000 bouquets across Mohali and Chandigarh, and we still pack every single one like it's going to someone we know.
